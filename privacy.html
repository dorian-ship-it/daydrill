DayDrill

Privacy Policy

DayDrill does not collect your data. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing to sign in to.

What the app stores, and where

Your practice history, your profile settings, your session plans and your timers are saved on your iPhone, in storage shared only between DayDrill and its own widgets and Watch app. That information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase everything from inside the app, in Settings.

Finding courts

If you search for courts, DayDrill sends the ZIP code you typed — or, only if you tap to use your location and grant permission, your approximate coordinates — to Apple's Maps service, which returns nearby results. That request goes to Apple, not to us. We never receive it and we never store your location.

Location access is optional. The ZIP code field works without it, and the rest of the app does not need it at all. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Links out of the app

The gear and courts sections contain links that open in Safari, on websites we do not run. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies to what happens there. We receive nothing about what you do after you leave.

What we do not do

  • No analytics.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking, across apps or anywhere else.
  • No third-party code of any kind is built into DayDrill.

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to us, because none of it reaches us in the first place.

Children

DayDrill does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of DayDrill ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy details will change with it.
