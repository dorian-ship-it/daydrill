Common questions

Does DayDrill work without signal?

Yes. The drills, the session generator, the timers, your history and the rules all work with no connection at all. The only feature that needs signal is the court finder, because it asks Apple's Maps service where the courts are.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and there never will be. Everything lives on your phone.

How do I erase my data?

More, then Settings, then Erase everything. Deleting the app does the same thing.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only to look for public courts near you, and only if you tap that button. The ZIP code field is the main path and works without granting anything. Your location is never stored and never sent to us.

Is it really free?

Yes. No purchases, no subscription, no ads.

Something is wrong with a drill or a rule

Tell me. The rules reflect the 2026 USA Pickleball rulebook and the drills are written from scratch, but if something reads wrong I would rather fix it than leave it.
